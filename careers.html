02 · Process

Fast, respectful, and focused on real work.

How to apply

We review every submission personally — show us what you have built, written, or designed.

STEP 01

Send Your Work

Share your CV alongside links to real projects, GitHub repositories, live sites, papers, or design portfolios at careers@anyatechnology.com.

STEP 02

Technical Conversation

A direct 45-minute discussion with our core team walking through a system or product you built and how you approach hard trade-offs.

STEP 03

Practical Collaboration

A scoped, realistic design or engineering exercise mirroring actual Anya Tech work — followed by a fast, transparent decision.

Recruiting Notice: All official hiring communications from Anya Tech are sent exclusively from @anyatechnology.com email addresses. We never ask candidates for fees or sensitive financial details during the application process.
